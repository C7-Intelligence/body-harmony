⏱️ Half-Life Reference

Compare plasma half-life (time to clear from blood) with biological effect duration (how long therapeutic effects persist).

Key Insight: For many peptides, there is a distinct difference between plasma half-life (the time it takes for half the intact peptide to clear from the bloodstream) and biological effect half-life (how long effects persist). Direct receptor agonists like Semaglutide have effects that closely match their plasma profile, while bioregulators like BPC-157 and Epithalon act as "molecular switches" — their effects last days to weeks after they've cleared.

Understanding the Difference

🎯

Direct Receptor Agonists

Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, Retatrutide: Their biological effect highly correlates with plasma half-life. They're engineered to stay in the blood a long time to keep receptors constantly active.

🔬

Bioregulators & Signaling Peptides

BPC-157, GHK-Cu, Epithalon, Selank: They exit the bloodstream incredibly fast but act as "molecular switches." Once they flip the switch (altering gene expression, releasing growth factors), the body stays in that altered state for hours, days, or even weeks.

About This Data

Half-life data is compiled from published pharmacokinetic studies, clinical trial data, and community consensus. Biological effect duration is approximate and can vary significantly between individuals.
