🏪 Vendor Directory

Peptide vendors, supplier types, testing resources, and medical supply sources. These are NOT endorsements — shop at your own risk.

⚠ IMPORTANT — Single Vial Sellers are NOT Recommended:
1. Single vials are inherently untestable — you have no idea what you're receiving.
2. With kits, you can test 1 vial at random for reasonable confidence — especially with group testing.
3. Single vials are usually 2–10x more expensive per vial than kits.
4. Single vial sellers have been caught faking COAs or rebranding vendor COAs.
5. Single vial sellers are often individuals with higher exit scam risk.
6. Single vial sellers add another middleman — compounding vendor AND reseller issues.
