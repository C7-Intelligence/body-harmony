Growth Hormone (GH) Secretion Pathway

The Hypothalamus releases GHRH (stimulatory) and SST (inhibitory) which act on the Anterior Pituitary. The Stomach releases Ghrelin, which also stimulates GH secretion. GH acts on Adipose tissues (releasing Free Fatty Acids) and on the Liver (producing IGF-I). IGF-I provides negative feedback. Factors like Stress, Amino Acids, and Hypoglycemia stimulate the Hypothalamus.
