🔬 Impact on Research Chemicals

☁️

Cloudy Appearance

Particulates and haze can appear after reconstitution, indicating poor solvent quality and potential instability.

🧊

Gel Formation

Improper pH or preservative levels can cause peptides to form gels, affecting handling and analysis.

⚗️

Aggregation / Precipitation

Peptides may aggregate or precipitate, leading to loss of solubility, reduced bioactivity, and unreliable data.

⚠️

Degradation / Instability

pH deviations and low BA content can accelerate degradation, shorten shelf life, and alter research chemical behavior.
