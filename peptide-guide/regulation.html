⚖️ Regulatory Timeline

Expanded timeline of FDA regulatory shifts, major legal actions, and international policy changes affecting peptide access — from late 2023 through July 2026.

⚠ Key Context: The FDA placed 19 popular peptides (like BPC-157) on the Category 2 bulk list, restricting compounding pharmacies. In February 2026, HHS announced intentions to reconsider these restrictions. In April 2026, the FDA officially removed multiple peptides from Category 2 restrictions after nominations were withdrawn.
Late 2023 – 2024
FDA Category 2 Restrictions
The FDA placed 19 popular peptides (including BPC-157) on the Category 2 bulk list, restricting compounding pharmacies from producing them.
Source: FDA Updates
Mid-2024
Novo Nordisk Lawsuits
Filed multiple lawsuits against med spas, clinics, and pharmacies for unlawful marketing and sales of compounded drugs claiming to contain semaglutide.
Source: Novo Nordisk Statement
June 2024
Eli Lilly Lawsuits Begin
Launched lawsuits against compounding pharmacies and medical spas for allegedly selling unapproved and counterfeit versions of tirzepatide.
Source: Duane Morris Review
January 2025
FDA Revised Interim Policies
Enforced revised interim policies to further limit bulk substance compounding and favor formal drug approval pathways.
Source: Peptide Drug Summit
March 2025
China NMPA Biological Product Guidelines
Enforced new Biological Product Registration Guidelines, mandating strict electronic declaration formats for therapeutic biologics.
Source: Cisema
April 2025
Eli Lilly Targets Telehealth
Expanded legal campaign by suing major telehealth platforms (Henry Meds, Mochi Health) for prescribing and distributing unauthorized GLP-1 drugs.
Source: HK Law Insights
May 2025
India CDSCO Similar Biologics Guidelines
Published revised Guidelines on Similar Biologics, updating characterization and approval pathways for complex peptides and biosimilars.
Source: CDSCO Guidelines
July 2025
China NMPA Domestic Responsible Person Rule
Required overseas pharmaceutical companies to appoint a Domestic Responsible Person bearing joint liability for imported therapeutic products.
Source: Arnold & Porter
Late 2025
India Approves Dual Agonists
Approved novel dual-agonist peptide therapeutics (e.g., tirzepatide) while simultaneously banning 35 irrational fixed-dose combinations.
Source: ResearchGate
January 2026
Antitrust Lawsuit Against Big Pharma
Strive Compounding Pharmacy filed a federal antitrust lawsuit against Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk, alleging they unlawfully pressured the market to boycott compounded GLP-1s.
Source: PR Newswire
February 2026
HHS Reconsiders Category 2
The Department of Health and Human Services announced intentions to reconsider the restrictive Category 2 status for most restricted peptides.
Source: Amanecia Health
March 2026
China NMPA Toxicology Updates
Implemented updated toxicological testing methods, including mandatory in-vitro kinetic direct peptide reactivity assays for cosmetics.
Source: EnterCo Group
April 2026
FDA Removes Category 2 Restrictions
Officially removed multiple peptides from Category 2 restrictions after their initial nominations for restriction were withdrawn. HGH Import Seizure also reported.
Source: BSCG Review, FDA Import Alert
May 2026
India CDSCO GLP-1 Enforcement
Issued formal public notice strengthening enforcement against unauthorized online promotion and gray-market distribution of GLP-1 weight-loss peptides.
Source: CDSCO Notices
June 2026
GLP-1 MDL Surpasses 3,700 Cases
Multidistrict litigation (MDL) against Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly regarding severe GLP-1 side effects (gastroparesis) surpassed 3,700 active cases.
Source: Consumer Notice
July 2026 (Scheduled)
PCAC Review of Removed Peptides
The Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee is set to review the removed peptides to determine if they can return to the legal 503A compounding list.
Source: OptiMantra Report
